Impact of the lunar cycle on the incidence of aneurysmal subarachnoid haemorrhage: myth or reality?

To investigate the impact of the lunar cycle on the incidence of aneurysmal subarachnoid haemorrhages. We retrospectively identified all patients admitted to the department of neurosurgery during 1992 and 2004 suffering from aneurysmal subarachnoid haemorrhage. The onset of bleeding was compared with the lunar phase. We did not observe any significant impact of the lunar cycle on the incidence of aneurysmal subarachnoid haemorrhage in 717 consecutive patients (p=0.84). The impact of the lunar cycle on aneurysmal subarachnoid haemorrhage is a myth rather than reality.
